\label{sec:demo}

A live demo of the MemeSonic pipeline—including meme input, incongruity score visualization,
and synthesized audio playback—is available at:

\section{Error Analysis}
\label{sec:error_analysis}

\textbf{Where Incongruity Fusion fails.}
Incongruity Fusion underperforms on MET/intention ($-15.6\%$ F1 vs.\ Unimodal-Image) and
Memotion/humour ($-8.9\%$ F1 vs.\ Late Fusion). In both cases, the incongruity signal introduces
noise when the task requires holistic understanding rather than conflict exploitation.
Intention classification (interactive, entertaining, etc.) is determined by distributional
properties of the image-text pair, not their emotional contrast. Future work could gate the
incongruity feature adaptively per task rather than applying it uniformly.

\textbf{LLMs and reasoning models.}
The strong performance of o4-mini (CoT) on Memotion/offensive (59.9\% F1) and sarcasm (44.0\% F1)
suggests that chain-of-thought reasoning provides substantial benefit for tasks requiring social
context—an advantage unavailable to our CLIP-based trained models. Combining LLM reasoning with
our incongruity signal (e.g., as a structured prompt feature) is a promising direction.

\textbf{Emotion2Vec vs.\ ImageBind for audio.}
ImageBind is trained on environmental sounds and achieves effectively zero performance on TTS
speech alignment ($A \leftrightarrow T$ Top-1 $\approx$ 0.00 in our probing). Emotion2Vec
captures sentiment-discriminative structure but not prosodic style variation. Neither model is
ideal for meme voiceover; a model trained on emotionally annotated TTS speech would be needed
for complete tri-modal integration without relying on a learned projector.
