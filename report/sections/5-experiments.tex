\section{Experimental Methodology}

\subsection{Datasets}

We evaluate on three benchmarks spanning ten tasks (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_stats}).
\textbf{MET-Meme}~\citep{metmeme} covers intention, sentiment, hate, and metaphor classification
(\textasciitilde1,400/300/340 train/val/test).
\textbf{Memotion 7k}~\citep{chhavi2020memotion} covers humour, sarcasm, offensiveness,
motivational, and sentiment tasks (random 80/10/10 split, seed 42).
\textbf{MMSD 2.0}~\citep{mmsd2} is a binary sarcasm detection benchmark
(16,000/3,000/3,482 official split).
All datasets provide meme images paired with overlaid text captions as input.

\subsection{Baselines}

\textbf{Phase 1.} Vanilla TTS (no affect conditioning) and Label-Guided TTS (discrete label
$\to$ fixed prosody mapping) serve as baselines for MemeSonic.

\textbf{Phase 2.} Trained baselines: Unimodal-Image, Unimodal-Text, Concat Fusion, Late Fusion,
MULT. LLM zero-shot baselines: GPT-4o, Gemini-2.5-Flash, o4-mini (CoT),
Gemini-2.5-Flash (CoT), Qwen3-VL-30B (Thinking). Our methods: Incongruity Fusion (Expert-S
in isolation) and FusionMoE (full 3-expert system).

\textbf{Phase 3.} For each of 100 memes, 9 audio variants are synthesized by sweeping ElevenLabs
prosody settings over a $3\times3$ grid (stability $\times$ style $\in \{0.2,0.5,0.8\}^2$),
giving 900 files. Emotion2Vec embeddings are extracted and a linear projector is trained
to align audio into a shared affective space (80/20 split).

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}

\textbf{Phase 1.} Human MOS on three axes (12 raters, 84 ratings): \textbf{NMOS} (naturalness),
\textbf{EMOS} (expressiveness), \textbf{AMOS} (pragmatic appropriateness). Pairwise A/B
preference tests complement the MOS scores.

\textbf{Phases 2 \& 3.} Macro-F1 (primary) and Accuracy.
